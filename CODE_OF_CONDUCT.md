# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free, inclusive project. Participants must be respectful, constructive, and considerate; avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and publication of private financial or credential data.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or disruptive. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in SUPPORT.md. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and project-related communications.
